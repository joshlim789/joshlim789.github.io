Gymnastics:

At UCLA, I served as president[link to athlete website https://uclaclubsports.com/sports/gymnastics/roster/joshua-lim/14062] from 2022 to 2024. In my last year competing at UCLA, we notched our school's very first team national title. Upon arriving at Duke, I helped found our school's club team [link https://dukechronicle.com/article/duke-club-gymnastics-new-club-peralta-ayodele-josh-lim-ucla-the-den-jordan-chiles-naigc-triangle-collaboration-unity-facility-20250422] and continue to train and compete. 

I am also a huge fan of the legendary UCLA Gymnastics Team, [https://www.latimes.com/sports/ucla/story/2023-03-28/ucla-gymnastics-super-fan] and I regularly venture back to watch their floor parties in Pauley Pavilion.
